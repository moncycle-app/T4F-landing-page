Tech 4 Fertility is a French non-profit association **putting technology to work for natural family planning**. This section is where we will keep you up to date with what is happening, as it happens.

## What you will find here

- **New releases** of our projects, starting with [moncycle.app](https://www.moncycle.app/), with what changes in practice for you;
- **new feature announcements**, sometimes before they ship, so you know what is coming;
- **changes within the association**: organisation, team, governance, new projects;
- **write-ups of our events** and meetings;
- **in-depth articles** on innovation in reproductive health.

## No pressure on the pace

We have no fixed schedule: we publish when we have something useful to say, never to fill a page. Every post is available in both French and English.

## How to follow along

You can come back to this page from time to time, or subscribe to the **RSS feed** (the link is at the bottom of every page) to be notified as soon as a post is published. A question, an idea, a topic you would like us to cover? Write to us from the contact section of the home page.

Thank you for following us, and see you soon for the next news.
